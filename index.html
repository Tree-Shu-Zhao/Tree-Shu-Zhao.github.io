Curiosity drives me to keep building on what I know and connecting ideas across fields. My research began in computer vision during my master's, grew to include multimodal learning during my Ph.D., and now focuses on LLM agents at NVIDIA. At the moment, I'm particularly interested in how post-training can turn LLMs into agents that get real work done.

Outside of work, I love driving and taking in the scenery. My curiosity turned that into a side project: Joi (named after Blade Runner 2049), an AI co-pilot that runs on a Jetson Nano in my car, gives me road and safety information, and chats with me along the way.

I don't know where my curiosity will take me next, and that's part of the fun.
